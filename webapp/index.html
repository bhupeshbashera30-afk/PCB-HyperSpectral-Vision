Component Taxonomy

The system classifies each pixel region into one of four component categories.

0

Others

Background substrate, traces, solder mask, and non-component regions.

1

IC

Integrated Circuits — QFP, BGA, SOP and other semiconductor packages.

2

Capacitor

Ceramic, electrolytic, and tantalum capacitors for energy storage.

3

Connector

Pin headers, edge connectors, USB ports and I/O interfaces.
